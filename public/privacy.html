Tasbeeh — Dhikr & Du'a Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

This app collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of its own. Your counts, favourites and settings never leave your device.

What is stored, and where

Everything the app remembers is written to your browser or device storage on the device itself:

  • your dhikr and du'a counts, day by day
  • your favourites, pinned items, custom du'as and collections
  • your settings — theme, language, font size, sound and haptics

None of this is transmitted anywhere. We cannot see it, and we have no way to recover it if you clear your device data — which is why the app has a Backup & Restore feature that saves a file under your own control.

What is not collected

  • No name, email address, phone number, or account of any kind
  • No location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking libraries
  • No record of what you recite or how often

Network connections

The app is designed to work fully offline and makes only one optional network request: when you choose to add a surah to your personal collection, it fetches that surah's text from the public Quran API at api.alquran.cloud. No personal information is sent with that request. If you never add a surah, the app makes no network requests at all after it is installed.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Since no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes it will be updated on this page, and the change will be visible in the app's public source history.
